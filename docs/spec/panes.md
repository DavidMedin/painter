# Panes
Out of scope: Panes across multiple OS windows.
## Definitions
- **Pane**: A rectangular region within the Application window.
- **Splitter**: The area between a pane and other panes.
## Requirements

PANE-1 - The system shall not allow panes to overlap.
PANE-2 - The border of every pane shall have a splitter.
PANE-3 - Splitters have a visual handle, in the center of it, that when dragged resizes the panes it borders.
PANE-4 - Every pane shall have a toolbar over the whole top edge.
PANE-5 - Every pane toolbar shall have a button on the far right that closes the pane.

PANE-6 - The system shall allow each pane to be in one Pane Mode at a time.
PANE-7 - The system shall have a dropdown on the far left of the pane toolbar that determines the pane's mode.
PANE-8 - The Pane mode determines the content in the content area of the pane.
